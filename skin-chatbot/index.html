The brief

Skincare advice, without a shop assistant

Online, customers can't ask a beauty advisor which products suit their skin. The brand wanted to give that advice automatically, from a simple photo.

Photo skin analysis

The customer sends a photo in the chat. An AI vision model detects the skin type and concerns such as acne-prone skin, fine lines or dryness.

Advisor chatbot

The chatbot asks a few questions, combines the answers with the analysis and recommends the matching products from the brand's range.

How it works

From photo to recommendation

  1. Photo and questionsThe customer sends a photo of their skin and answers a few questions: how the skin feels, main concerns.
  2. AI analysisAn AI vision model detects the skin type and concerns on the photo.
  3. Cross-checkThe answers confirm what the photo shows, so the result never depends on the photo alone.
  4. RecommendationThe chatbot recommends the products from the brand's range that match the skin profile.
  5. Photo deletedOnce the analysis is done, the photo is deleted. It is not kept.

The hard part

Photos aren't reliable

Problem

The same skin could get a different result depending on the lighting and the quality of the photo. Recommending products from the photo alone wasn't reliable enough.

My fix

I added a few questions to the chat (how the skin feels, main concerns) and combined the answers with the photo analysis. The recommendation never relies on the photo alone.

Security

Privacy by design

Skin photos are personal data

Each photo is deleted after the analysis and never kept. Coming from a bank's security operations centre, I decide where the data goes before I write the first line of code.

Result

Delivered for real customers

  • Customers get personalised product advice from one photo and a few questions.
  • Delivered end to end as a freelancer: interface, back end and AI integration.

Next

What I'd improve

  • Check photo quality first: if a photo is too dark or blurry, ask for a new one before the analysis.
  • Measure the impact: track how many recommendations end in a purchase.
